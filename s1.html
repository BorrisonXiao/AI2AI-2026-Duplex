The reproduction loop

We use the authors’ native prompt style: a patient assistant with short, clear speech. Generation is greedy (temperature=0), and each raw model reply is retained in conversation history. The input feeder releases 480 ms slices on a fixed clock; Thinker requests run sequentially while ordered speech synthesis and 20 ms playback run concurrently.

The author-harness factual and repeat examples use one A100-SXM4 80 GB. Thinker, Talker, MTP, and Code2Wav all share GPU 0. The human dialogue pair uses one H100 SXM 80 GB, with the same components on GPU 0. S2 is absent from both setups. The runs are sequential, with a maximum of one GPU active.

A clarification during speech

The human request asks about an outlet versus a regular store. In the clarification branch, the same speaker adds “Faulty products?” during the model’s answer. S1 emits its native [STOP] at tick 13. The controller clears 0.28 seconds of queued audio and rejects obsolete synthesis before playing the new reply.

Measured human/model speech overlap is 0.40 seconds; the stop arrives 0.492 seconds after nonquiet human onset. The first 12 field ticks match the uninterrupted branch. Independent transcription of played audio verifies the cut and the subsequent clarification response. These are selected short interaction examples.

Audio and model sources

The factual and repeat inputs are authored prompts synthesized with Flite SLT; Flite license. The outlet and clarification inputs come from DailyTalk by Keon Lee, Kyumin Park and Daeyoung Kim, conversation 656, speaker/channel 1, turns 2 and 4. Channel extraction, cropping, resampling and barge-in timing are adaptations under CC BY-SA 4.0. The source counterpart’s answer is excluded from model input.
